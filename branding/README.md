# AVEE VPN brand pack

Master artwork is in `svg/`. Raster exports are in `png/`, the Google Play assets are in `play-store/`, and website browser icons are in `favicon/`.

## Recommended usage

- Android launcher / Play icon: `play-store/app-icon-512.png` or the vector master `svg/avee-mark-dark.svg`.
- Google Play feature graphic: `play-store/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`.
- Dark website or app lockup: `png/avee-vpn-lockup-dark-1600x900.png`.
- Light website or document lockup: `png/avee-vpn-lockup-light-1600x900.png`.
- Header wordmark: `png/avee-wordmark-dark-1200x360.png` or the light variant.
- Browser tab: `favicon/favicon.svg`; PNG fallbacks are provided at 16, 32, 48, and 180 px.

The website `index.html` references `./favicon.svg` and the PNG fallbacks in its local `favicon/` folder.
